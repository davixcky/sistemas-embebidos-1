Independent analog simulation comparison, 2026-10-23

Executed: XyceNF Release 7.10.0 and ngspice 46.
18 existing cases plus 6 temperature sensitivity cases.
Results: resultados.txt (readable) and resultados.json (numeric).
Generated .cir decks, .log files and waveforms are retained alongside results.

Build the isolated Xyce image (Docker required):
  mkdir -p /tmp/c2-abpr-xyce
  cp simulacion/xyce/Dockerfile /tmp/c2-abpr-xyce/Dockerfile
  curl -fL 'https://xyce.sandia.gov/download/1984/?tmstv=1754506753' -o /tmp/c2-abpr-xyce/xyce.rpm
  sha256sum /tmp/c2-abpr-xyce/xyce.rpm
  docker build -t c2-abpr-xyce:7.10 /tmp/c2-abpr-xyce
  python3 herramientas/comparar_simuladores.py

Interpretation:
The baseline solvers agree on the extracted voltage metrics within 10 microvolts.
The 4.75-V supply case fails the project's 4.4-V regulator-input criterion in both.
The added 0-C and 15-C overload cases fail the stated -0.3..3.6-V limits in both.
Temperature behavior uses the original fitted diode model, including its default
temperature parameters; it is NOT a manufacturer-qualified BAT85 temperature model.
Actual parts may differ. No schematic or component values were changed.

This is an independent numerical solver check, not independent physical model
validation. Both use the same ideal CT, ideal 3.3-V rail and fitted diode models.
The regulator itself, PTC heating/tripping, USB power path, ADC sampling and real
ZMPT amplifier are not simulated. The mains assembly and insulation are not validated.
